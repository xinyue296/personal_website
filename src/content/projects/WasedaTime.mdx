[WasedaTime](https://wasedatime.com/) is an open-source, student-run web application designed to support student life at Waseda University in Tokyo, Japan. It integrates essential tools like class timetable organization, course review searches, and community updates into a single web portal.

## My Roles

I contributed to the project from **February 2022 to August 2023** in a dual role as a **Frontend Developer** and **Product Manager**.

## Key Contributions

### Frontend Development
* **Responsive UI/UX**: Designed and developed responsive interfaces using **TypeScript**, **React**, and **Tailwind CSS**. I focused on ensuring that timetables and course dashboards are fully accessible across both desktop and mobile layouts.
* **Component Design**: Translated design wireframes from **Figma** into pixel-perfect, reusable React components, ensuring high-fidelity implementation.
* **API Integration**: Collaborated closely with backend developers to integrate rest APIs for dynamic course searching and calendar loading.

### Product & Community Management
* **Agile Planning**: Led weekly team syncs, aligned engineering milestones with student feedback, and organized sprints to coordinate release schedules.
* **Student Outreach**: Crafted engaging social media visuals on **Canva** for marketing, driving a **20% increase in active student sessions** and registrations.

## Technologies Used

* **TypeScript & React**: Built stable, type-safe client-side interactions.
* **Tailwind CSS**: Designed a responsive, modern layout without custom stylesheet bloat.
* **Figma & Canva**: Utilized for wireframing user journeys and designing marketing assets.

## Conclusion

Working on WasedaTime was my first experience contributing to a large-scale, student-run web application. It helped me bridge UI/UX design principles with front-end development, while fostering a collaborative product-focused mindset when working alongside backend developers.
